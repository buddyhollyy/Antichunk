How to find map.sqlite
  1. Pick your computer:
  3. Click “Open your world…”. In the window that opens, click the address bar at the top, paste, and press Enter.
  4. Open the folder with your world's name and pick map.sqlite.

Close the world in Luanti first, so everything is saved. You can also drag map.sqlite onto the map.
